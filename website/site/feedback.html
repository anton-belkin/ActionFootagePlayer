Open source, GPL-3.0. Built with thanks on Gyroflow's stabilization engine and Adrian Eddy's telemetry-parser, with the D-Log M and GP-Log HDR LUTs derived from Zeb Gardner's shaper LUTs (used with permission), and the log curves published by DJI, GoPro and Insta360. The app bundles mpv and ffmpeg under their own licenses. Not affiliated with DJI, GoPro or Insta360.
