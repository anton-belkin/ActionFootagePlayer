Action Footage Library

Open a trip

A project is a trip folder with Project.afproject in it. Double-click that file, drop it here, or choose it with Open….

No project yet? Open one of the trip's clips in ActionFootagePlayer and initialize the project in its side panel's Project tab.

This project is from before 0.9: the player moves its _ActionFootagePlayer folder into Project.afproject the next time it opens one of its clips.

Clip previews

A flipbook of each clip in color, in _Clips: the trip at a glance in Finder. Only for clips without one; a removed one stays removed.

    Moments of all marks

    Every mark that's in no moment yet becomes one, in _Moments: 2 s on each side, marks close together joined.

    Photos of all marks

    A photo at each mark, into _Stills, with the clip's saved view. Once each: a deleted one isn't made again.

    Export all moments

    Each moment not exported yet, into _Exports, with its own settings, as Shift+E exports one.

    Queue

    Nothing yet. Batches run one at a time, in the background; the player can play meanwhile.
